Privacy Policy — Warranty Keeper

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Warranty Keeper is designed to help users record, organize, and manage product warranties. Premium users can also attach photos and PDF documents as proof of purchase or warranty documentation.

Information Entered by the User

Warranty Keeper allows users to enter information related to their warranties, such as product names, purchase dates, warranty expiration dates, store names, and notes.

Premium users can also select photos and PDF files from their device and associate them with their warranty records.

Data Storage

Warranty information and associated documents are stored locally on the user's device.

Warranty Keeper does not require users to create an account and does not operate its own online storage service for warranty information, photos, or PDF documents.

Photos and Files

When a user chooses to attach proof of purchase or other warranty documentation, Warranty Keeper may request access to photos or allow the user to select a PDF file.

This access is used only to provide the feature requested by the user. Warranty Keeper does not deliberately upload these photos or documents to servers operated by the developer.

In-App Purchases

Warranty Keeper offers an optional Premium upgrade as a one-time in-app purchase.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. Warranty Keeper does not receive or store the user's credit card details or other payment credentials.

Data Sharing

Warranty Keeper does not sell user data.

The application does not deliberately transmit warranty records, notes, photos, or PDF documents to servers operated by the developer.

Purchase transactions are processed by Google Play and are subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

Data Deletion

Users can delete warranty records and their associated information from within the application.

Uninstalling Warranty Keeper also removes locally stored application data, subject to backup and restore mechanisms provided by the user's operating system or device.

Children's Privacy

Warranty Keeper is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Warranty Keeper is designed to keep warranty information and attached documents locally on the user's device. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and any device backups.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change.
